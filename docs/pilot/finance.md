# eira Pilot — Finance

## Status

Working document. Financial assumptions should be replaced with observed costs as the pilot develops.

## Objective

The pilot should be economically understandable from the beginning. The goal is not simply to minimise expenditure, but to understand what capabilities cost and whether the model can become sustainable and reproducible.

## Cost categories

Initial categories include:

- devices
- shared computers and compute
- networking and internet connectivity
- power and energy infrastructure
- software and cloud inference
- maintenance and replacement
- transport and logistics
- physical space and equipment
- training and support
- locally purchased materials

## Questions to measure

- capital cost per participant
- recurring cost per participant
- inference cost per active user
- connectivity cost
- expected device lifetime
- repair and replacement cost
- cost of shared infrastructure relative to personal hardware
- which costs can be sourced locally
- which costs fall as the pilot scales

## Principle

Financial sustainability is part of the system architecture. eira should aim for sufficient capability and quality without assuming permanently subsidised high-cost infrastructure or inference.
