
# Initial Pilot

The first implementation of Eira will begin as a pilot project in **Mumford, Ghana**.

Our objective is to establish a partnership with the headteacher and one classroom teacher at a local primary school.

The initial pilot will focus on **one Upper Primary classroom (Basic 4–6, approximately 9–11 years of age).**

This age group represents a suitable balance between learner independence and educational need.

The pilot will allow Eira to be evaluated in a real classroom environment before expanding to additional classes within Mumford. Particular attention will be given to:

- integration into everyday teaching,
- support for homework and independent learning,
- teacher feedback,
- student engagement,
- educational outcomes, and
- technical reliability in a local-first environment.

The lessons learned from the pilot will guide the future development of Eira, with the long-term goal of supporting learners across **primary and secondary education** in Mumford.


## Initial Subject

The initial pilot will focus on **Mathematics**.

Mathematics was selected because it provides a clear and structured curriculum, well-defined learning objectives, and objective methods for evaluating student progress. It also offers a strong foundation for assessing Eira's ability to provide accurate, curriculum-aligned educational support.

Beginning with a single subject allows the project to focus on educational quality, classroom integration, and technical reliability before expanding to additional subjects.

Following successful evaluation of the mathematics pilot, Eira will gradually be extended to other core subjects in the Ghanaian curriculum.
