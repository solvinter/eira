# ADR-0007 – Initial Subject Selection: Mathematics

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

The initial pilot of Eira will be conducted in a single Upper Primary class (Basic 4–6) in Mumford, Ghana. To minimise complexity while enabling meaningful evaluation, the pilot will initially focus on a single academic subject.

## Decision

The first subject implemented in the pilot will be **Mathematics**.

## Rationale

Mathematics was selected based on the following considerations:

- **Objectivity** – Mathematical problems generally have clearly defined correct answers, making student performance straightforward to assess.
- **Curriculum Alignment** – Learning objectives are well structured and closely aligned with the Ghanaian curriculum, facilitating lesson planning and content development.
- **Evaluation** – Student learning outcomes can be measured objectively through pre- and post-pilot assessments, allowing the educational impact of Eira to be evaluated.
- **Scalability** – The lessons learned from the mathematics pilot will provide a foundation for extending Eira to additional subjects in future iterations.

## Consequences

Restricting the pilot to mathematics reduces implementation complexity and allows development efforts to focus on educational quality, teacher integration, and system evaluation. The knowledge gained during the pilot will inform future expansion to other subjects within the Ghanaian primary school curriculum.
