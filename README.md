# Eira

> **Eira is not a language model.**

Eira is an open-source AI platform that orchestrates language models, retrieval systems, speech technologies, and educational resources to provide reliable, locally deployable educational assistance.

## Vision

Eira is designed to support education and community development through open-source, locally deployable AI. Rather than replacing teachers, Eira is intended to empower teachers, learners, and communities by making high-quality educational assistance available even where Internet connectivity is limited or unavailable.
